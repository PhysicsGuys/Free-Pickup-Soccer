1. Participant information

2. Acknowledgment of risks

I understand that soccer is a physical activity with inherent risks. These may include collisions; falls, trips, or slips on grass or uneven surfaces; sprains, strains, bruises, fractures, head injuries or concussions; accidental contact; dehydration, exhaustion, heat-related illness; and injuries from running, kicking, jumping, or changing direction. Injuries can occur even when participants follow rules and reasonable safety precautions are taken.

3. Participant safety and physical matching

Organizers may deny, pause, restrict, or discontinue participation when they reasonably believe that a participant's physical capabilities, size relative to other players, behavior, health, or playing ability creates a safety concern. This may be to protect other players from an unreasonable risk or to protect the participant from an increased risk of injury due to a significant physical mismatch or other safety concern.

Any decision involving size or physical matching will be based on an individualized, reasonable safety assessment, not size alone. Organizers may separate players into groups, modify activities, or require a participant to stop playing when reasonably necessary for safety.

4. Rules and conduct

My child is expected to follow reasonable organizer instructions; treat others respectfully; avoid dangerous or aggressive play, fighting, threats, and bullying; respect the playing area and property; and stop playing when instructed for safety. Organizers may remove a participant for dangerous conduct, repeated rule violations, or behavior that threatens another participant's safety.

5. Health and medical responsibility

I confirm, to the best of my knowledge, that my child is able to participate in recreational soccer, and I will inform organizers of relevant medical restrictions or conditions. Organizers are not providing medical supervision or professional medical services. In an emergency, I authorize organizers to contact emergency services and request appropriate medical assistance. If there is no additional information, write N/A above.

6. Supervision and transportation

I understand this is an informal recreational activity. Unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing, organizers are not providing childcare, transportation, or continuous individual supervision. I am responsible for deciding whether my child is ready to participate and arranging safe transportation. Organizers may cancel or stop a game due to unsafe weather, field conditions, insufficient supervision, or other safety concerns.

7. Field and equipment safety

Participants should wear suitable athletic clothing and footwear. Shin guards are recommended. Participants must report unsafe field conditions, damaged equipment, or injuries to an organizer. Organizers may stop play if conditions become unsafe.

8. Release, Waiver, and Covenant Not to Sue

In consideration of my child being permitted to participate in this free activity, and to the fullest extent permitted by New Jersey law, I, on behalf of myself and my child, release, waive, discharge, and agree not to sue the organizers, their parents or legal guardians, and any volunteers (the "Released Parties") for any claims for injury, illness, death, or property damage arising out of or related to my child's participation, including claims based on the ordinary negligence of the Released Parties.

This release does not apply to gross negligence or to reckless, willful, or intentional misconduct.

I understand that New Jersey courts may not enforce a parent's release of a minor's own claims. If any part of this release is found unenforceable, it shall still apply to my own claims (including claims for my child's medical expenses) and to all other claims to the fullest extent permitted by law.

Severability. If any provision of this agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full effect.

9. Parent/guardian acknowledgment and consent

I have read this agreement and understand that soccer involves inherent risks. I give permission for my child to participate and understand the safety expectations and organizers' authority to make reasonable, individualized safety decisions. I understand that the release in Section 8 applies only to the extent permitted by law and does not release any party from liability that cannot legally be waived.
