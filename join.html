Free Pickup Soccer — Registration & Parent/Guardian Agreement

For players ages 11–15. Participation is free and games are planned for a grass field. Complete this form with a parent or guardian, print it, and bring the signed copy to the organizer.

1. Participant information

2. Acknowledgment of risks

I understand that soccer is a physical activity with inherent risks. These may include collisions with other players; falls, trips, or slips on grass or uneven surfaces; sprains, strains, bruises, fractures, head injuries or concussions; accidental contact; dehydration, exhaustion, and heat-related illness; and injuries from running, kicking, jumping, or changing direction.

I understand that injuries can occur even when participants follow rules and reasonable safety precautions are taken. I have considered these risks before giving permission for my child to participate.

3. Participant safety and size / physical matching

The safety of all participants is a priority. Organizers may deny, pause, restrict, or discontinue a participant's participation when they reasonably believe that the participant's physical capabilities, size relative to other players, behavior, health, or playing ability creates a safety concern.

  • Protection of others: a participant may be held out or moved to a different group if an individualized assessment indicates an unreasonable risk of injury to other players.
  • Protection of the participant: a participant may be held out or moved to a different group if a significant physical mismatch or other condition may place that participant at increased risk.

Any decision involving size or physical matching will be based on an individualized, reasonable safety assessment, not size alone. Organizers may separate players into groups, modify activities, or require a participant to stop playing when reasonably necessary for safety. Organizers make the on-site participation decision.

4. Rules and conduct

My child is expected to follow reasonable organizer instructions; treat other participants respectfully; avoid intentional dangerous or aggressive play, fighting, threats, and bullying; respect the playing area and property; and stop playing when instructed for safety reasons. Organizers may remove a participant for dangerous conduct, repeated rule violations, or behavior that threatens another participant's safety.

5. Health and medical responsibility

I confirm, to the best of my knowledge, that my child is able to participate in recreational soccer, and I will inform organizers of relevant medical restrictions, allergies, or conditions that could affect safe participation. I understand organizers are not providing medical supervision or professional medical services. In an emergency, I authorize organizers to contact emergency services and request appropriate emergency medical assistance for my child. I understand that I may be responsible for medical expenses, subject to applicable law and insurance coverage.

6. Supervision, transportation, and schedule

I understand this is an informal recreational soccer activity. Unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing, organizers are not providing childcare, transportation, or continuous individual supervision. I am responsible for deciding whether my child is ready to participate and arranging safe transportation to and from the activity. Organizers may cancel, postpone, or stop a game due to unsafe weather, field conditions, insufficient supervision, or other safety concerns.

7. Field and equipment safety

Participants should wear suitable athletic clothing and footwear. Shin guards are recommended. Participants must use equipment appropriately and report unsafe field conditions, damaged equipment, or injuries to an organizer. Organizers may stop play if the playing surface or surrounding conditions become unsafe.

8. Parent/guardian participation acknowledgment

I acknowledge that I have read this agreement and understand the risks described above. I have had the opportunity to ask questions and understand the safety expectations. I understand that signing this agreement does not guarantee that my child will never be injured and does not eliminate legal rights or responsibilities that cannot legally be waived.

9. Parent/guardian consent

By signing below, I confirm that I am the parent or legal guardian of the participant; I give permission for my child to participate in Free Pickup Soccer; I understand the risks and safety expectations described in this agreement; I understand the organizers' authority to make reasonable, individualized safety decisions about participation; and I have provided accurate emergency contact information.
