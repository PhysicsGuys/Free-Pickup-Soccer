What to Bring

  • Water bottle
  • Comfortable athletic clothes
  • Soccer cleats or athletic shoes suitable for grass
  • Shin guards (recommended)
  • Your completed, parent/guardian-signed registration form

Label your belongings and take everything home with you.

Rules of Pickup Soccer

  • Respect every player, regardless of skill level.
  • Play fairly; no fighting, bullying, dangerous tackles, or intentional rough play.
  • Listen to reasonable organizer instructions and stop play when asked.
  • Call fouls honestly and settle disagreements calmly.
  • Keep the field and surrounding area clean.
  • Tell an organizer if someone is hurt or the field or equipment seems unsafe.

Organizers may pause play, change teams, or ask someone to stop playing when needed for safety.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Participation is free.

Who can play?

The activity is for players ages 11–14.

Do I need to be on a team?

No, but it is recommended that you have some skill.

When are games?

The schedule is currently TBD. Check with the organizer for updates before traveling.

Do I need to register?

Yes. Complete the registration form with a parent or guardian, have them sign it, and bring it when you arrive.

What if the weather is bad?

Check with the organizer before leaving. Games may be changed or canceled for unsafe weather or field conditions.

Are adults supervising?

This is an informal recreational activity. Review the parent/guardian agreement carefully; do not rely on youth organizers for childcare, medical care, or transportation. It is recommended that you stay at the park with your child and supervise. Remember, this is run by youth participants.
